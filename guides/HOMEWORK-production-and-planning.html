Homework — understand a production result

Draft Homework 8 assignments Updated 2026-09-06

Goal: finish one production run, explain which master data produced the result, change one condition and predict the difference, then connect planning to production.

Use the bamboo test family in Company 4100. Work in order and stop after any result you cannot explain. A surprising result is useful evidence; do not click through it or repair several values at once.

Warning

Change only test-item data. Do not change the shared BAM-RM or BAM-FG item groups or their defaults.

7. Optional investigation — `BAM-SLEEVE` Not started

Do this only after Homework 1–5. The earlier run added planning data for BAM-SLEEVE, reran planning, and still produced no planned supply. The authored records disagree about whether the cause was its order horizon or stock covering the requirement.

Record all four values in one sitting before changing anything:

Cluster and site on the item's planning row

Order-horizon days and displayed horizon date

On-hand and existing supply

Demand date and quantity

Then run MRP once and capture the BAM-SLEEVE result or its absence in Planned Orders cprrp1100m000, Planning ➔ Order Planning ➔ Planned Orders.

The four baseline readings plus the MRP result can establish the cause.
