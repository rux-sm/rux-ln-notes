Demand to Shipment via Planning

Draft 7 phases Updated 2026-08-27

Sales · Enterprise Planning · Procurement · Manufacturing · Warehousing

Objective: run the bamboo family down the long route of WG_01_Order_to_Shipment_Flowchart.html — a sales order that stock cannot cover, through MRP, across the transfer boundary, buying the components and building the finished item, to a confirmed shipment with on-hand verified down. Spine guide — it carries the sequence and the handovers. Each phase names the atomic guide that owns its detail and does not restate it.

The diagram is the level-1 view of this guide — five phases across six module lanes, master data through confirmed shipment. The YES rail along the top is a ship-from-stock order that never crosses the transfer boundary; everything below it is the route this guide walks. Its sessions and their routes are stated per phase below, not on the diagram.

The Item Order Plan is the progress indicator for phases 0–2; the order status is the indicator from phase 3 on. Planned ➔ Confirmed ➔ transferred ➔ real order ➔ received or completed ➔ shipped.

Troubleshooting

Phase
Symptom
What to check or do
0An item is absent from the Item Order PlanIt has no cluster row. Step 0.5. Not an error state; nothing will tell you
1LN refuses the item on the sales orderNo row for the order's sales office on the Sales Offices tab of Item (tcibd0501m100). Add it, then close and reopen the order session
1The demand does not appear in the Item Order PlanThe delivery date is beyond the Order Horizon date. Step 1.5 — and the date is not today plus your days
2The run plans the pair but not a componentThe component has no planning data of its own. Step 0.4 — MRP skips it silently
2A component has plan data and still produces nothingIt may be correctly absent — planning proposes supply only for what stock does not cover. Read its own Item Order Plan and on-hand
2The pegging tree is emptyPegging relations were not generated, or the demand is beyond the Pegging Horizon in Planning Parameters (cprpd0100m000)
3The transfer runs and nothing happensThe orders were still Planned. Step 3.3. There is no message for "nothing matched"
3"Buy-from BP cannot be determined for item …"No partner row on the item's Item - Business Partner tab. Steps 3.1–3.2. The failed proposal survives, so fix it and rerun
3Purchase Order Type is blankLN gives an error. This environment defaults IPO
4No inbound order appears after the transferCheck the purchase order's Release to Warehousing tab; Purchase Order Line Status (tdpur4534m000) names the activity a stuck line is waiting on
4whinh0032 at the receiptCapture the LOG detail before clearing it, and read Errors Logged and Receipt Message Log (whinh3530m000) afterwards
4The receipt confirms but on-hand does not moveWhether All Lines Final and All Lines Complete were both set, and whether you re-read the warehouse from 0.3
5The order will not releasePrinting Production Order Documents Mandatory in tisfc0100s000 may require Printed first
5The material line stays blockedRun Initiate Inventory Issue (tisfc0207m000) manually; if still blocked, record what you saw
5Status reaches Production Completed and on-hand does not moveThat is the No path at 5.5. Check Warehousing Orders (whinh2100m000) for an open production receipt order
6Advice greyed outYou are on the session toolbar. The advice actions live on the Outbound Lines toolbar — step 6.3
6The order will not adviseThe header Warehouse does not hold the stock. Recheck against 0.2
6The shipment will not confirmThe line was never picked, or the shipment was never frozen. Steps 6.4 and 6.5

Variants

Variant
What changes
Stock already covers the orderThe YES branch of the flowchart. Planning generates no planned orders, and phases 2–5 do not happen at all — go straight to SG-ship-from-stock.md. MRP still runs; whether it generates anything depends on demand against on-hand plus on-order
The finished item is in the wrong warehouse to ship fromInsert SG-transfer-item-between-warehouses.md between phases 5 and 6. On current evidence BAM-CHOP-PAIR is received and shipped at ADNA01, so this does not arise
Components already in stockPhase 3 transfers only the production side and phase 4 does not happen. This is the likely shape of a sleeve-only shortfall
Build by hand instead of from planningSkip step 3.6 and create the production order directly per SG-manual-production-order-existing-master-data.md phase 1. This is the only production path anyone here has walked — it is the fallback if step 3.6 fails
